Baptism Birthplace Records Wrong?

Are some baptism’s birthplaces wrong?

In baptism entries for two of the children of Carl Friedrich Gottlieb Krückeberg we have two different birthplaces given—​or are these no birthplaces but current residences? The birthplace for Carl Friedrich born in 1807 and the daughter born the next year is given only as "Berenbusch". In these records, there is a column for the "Name, Stand, Religion and Birthplace of the Father". However, for the 1807 birth of Carl Friedrich, this column contains "Carl Friedrich Gottlieb Krückeberg. Schneider u. Einlieger in n. 14 in Bärenbusch. Luth. Conf.", while for the the 1812 birth of daughter Carloline Dorothee Krückeberg it has "Carl Friedrich Gottlieb Krückeberg n. 18 in Bärenbusch Luth. Conf.".

So is "n. 14 Berenbsuch" and "n. 18 Berenbusch" the address at the time the child was born despite the column stating it is the "birthplace of the father"? The actual birth record of Carl Friedrich Gottlieb is of no help in answering this question since it only mentions Berenbusch.

Answer: The answer is given in the column "Wohnort der Eltern", which has the same address as that of the father.
