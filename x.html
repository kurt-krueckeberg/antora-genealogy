Sigla Explained

The general format for most of the sigla representing original records is LLL-E-YYYYx where:

  • LLL—​is one of the 3-letter location prefixes for a specific parish shown in the table below:

    3-letter Prefix Location Meaning

    BUC

    Buchholz

    Parish in Buchholz, "Germany"

    CIN

    Cincinnati

    Deutsche Evangelisch-Lutherische und Reformierte Kirche von Cincinnati (German Evangelical Lutheran and Reformed Church of Cincinnati)

    FRI

    Frille

    Parish in Frille, "Germany"

    PET

    Petzen

    Parish in Petzen, Schaumburg-Lippe

    DEC

    Decatur

    Immanuel Lutheran Church, Decatur, Indiana

    NDC

    Cincinnati

    Norddeutsche Evangelisch-Lutherische Kirche, Cincinnati (North German Lutheran Church of Cincinnati)

    WAL

    Wallensen

    Wallsensen Parish, "Germany"

    WIN

    Windheim

    Windheim Parish, "Germany"

  • E—​is a one-letter church ceremony code shown in the table below:

    Code Ceremony

    B

    Birth/Baptism

    C

    Confirmation

    M

    Marriage

    D

    Burial/Death

  • YYYY—​is the year of the ceremony

  • x—​is a,b,c, d, etc, representing separate indvidual ceremonies from one another.
