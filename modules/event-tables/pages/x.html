Carl Friedrich Krückeberg (b. 1807) Event Tables

Introduction

The document that tells us where the Krückebergs who settled in Adms county, Indiana, originate is the derived-church-records:FS-CFK.html, which tells us Carl Friedrich Krückeberg, who emigrated from Schaumburg-Lippe in 1850 and settled in Adams County, Indiana, waas born in the village of "Beerenbsuch" [sic] (more commonly spelled Berenbsuch) in the principality of Schaumburg-Lippe.

It goes to explain that his father, Carl Friedrich Gottlieb, was a shoemaker and his social status was Einlieger implying he and his family lived as tenents, at least at the time of the birth of Carl Friedrich, their 1st son and 2nd child. His mother was from the village of Evensen [sic] (or Evesen) located immediately south of Berenbusch. Evesen and Berenbusch are also the villages of his baptismal sponsors.

We know the closest Lutheran Church closest to Evesen and Berenbusch was Petzen (See: Meyer’s Gazetteer). Furthermore the bottom of the derived-church-records:FS-CFK.html mentions the family’s parish and its acting pastor:

Petzen d. 23sten September 1850 Pede vacanti der vikarirende Prediger

Fürstentum Schaumburg Lippe.

  1. Schwerdtmann von Bergkirchen.

This translates as:

Petzen, the 23rd of September 1850 During a pastoral vacancy the acting preacher

Principality of Schaumburg-Lippe

L\. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen (signatory)

The acting pastor for Petzen was L. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen, and Petzen was the parish where the Krückeberg were members, and where their and their ancestor’s original birth, baptism, confirmation, marriage and death and burial records are found.

As of November 30, 2025, we only have online access to the Petzen church registers prior to 1827, but those after 1827 are only is only available at the Lower Saxony State Archive in Bückeburg. So we must rely on the derived-church-records:FS-CFK.html because it states it is derived from the original Petzen church records. The derived-church-records:FS-CFK.html begins "Laut Angabe des hiesigen Kirchenbuchs…​" or "According to the local church register…​".

While we do have the two Krückeberg biographies who were founders of Immanuel Lutheran, Decatur, Indiana

  1. immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.html

  2. immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-02.html

neither mentions the source of their information. On the other hand, the derived-church-records:FS-CFK.html does. It begins: Laut Angabe des hiesigen Kirchenbuchs ("according to the local church records").

Furthermore the biography of immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-02.html, the first child of Carl Friedrich, gives the father’s name of "Carl Gottlieb" and not "Carl Friedrich".
