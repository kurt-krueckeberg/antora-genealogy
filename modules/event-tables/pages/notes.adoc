My view on the wrong name given in the death record of 1801: The pastor was relatively new, he
had not known Johann Heinrich Krückeberg. When he looked up the deceased Sophia's birth/baptism record,
he also had no father's name, because in her baptism the father is recorded as 'Krueckeberg'. And
those who came to tell him about her death, might also not have known his full name,
possibly he was always called 'Heinrich', and the first name was forgotten.
