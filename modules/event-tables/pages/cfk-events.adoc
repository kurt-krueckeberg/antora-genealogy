== Introduction

The xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[] tells us that his parents lived in
*Beerenbusch* [sic], more typically spelled *Berenbsuch*. His father's status
was *Einlieger* implyiung they lived as tenants at this address. His mother was from
Evensen [sic] (Evesen), which is just south of Berenbusch. Evesen and Beerenbusch are also the
villages of his baptismal sponsors.

We know the closest Lutheran Church closest to Evesen and Berenbusch was Petzen
(See:https://www.meyersgaz.org/place/10466052). Furthermore *Petzen* is mentioned at the bottom of the
xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[] as being the family's parish:

[quote]
____ 
Petzen d. 23sten September 1850
Pede vacanti
der vikarirende Prediger

Fürstentum Schaumburg Lippe.

L. Schwerdtmann von Bergkirchen.
____

This translates as:

[quote]
____
Petzen, the 23rd of September 1850  
During a pastoral vacancy  
the acting preacher  

Principality of Schaumburg-Lippe  

L. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen
(signatory)
____

The acting pastor for Petzen was L. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen. Petzen was the parish
were the Krückeberg were members, where their original birth, baptism, confirmation, marriage
and death and burial records are found.

But as of November 30, 2025, we don't have access online to Petzen church records after 1827.
The Petzen church register after 1827 is only available at the Lower Saxony State Archive in
Bückeburg. So we must turn to the record derived from the original Petzen church records: 
xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[], and two biographies of the founders of Immanuel Lutheran,
Decatur, Indiana:

. xref:immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[]
. xref:immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-02.adoc[]

The xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[] begins: Laut Angabe des hiesigen Kirchenbuchs ("according to the
local church records". The xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-02.adoc[] does not explicitly note its source of
information.
