== Introduction

The document that tells us where the Krückebergs who settled in Adms county, Indiana, originate 
is the xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[], which tells us Carl Friedrich Krückeberg, who emigrated from
Schaumburg-Lippe in 1850 and settled in Adams County, Indiana, waas born in the village of 
*"Beerenbsuch"* [sic]  (more commonly spelled Berenbsuch) in the principality of Schaumburg-Lippe.

It goes to explain that his father, Carl Friedrich Gottlieb, was a shoemaker and his social status was *Einlieger*
implying he and his family lived as tenents, at least at the time of the birth of Carl Friedrich, their 1^st^ 
son and 2^nd^ child.  His mother was from the village of Evensen [sic] (or Evesen) located immediately south of
Berenbusch. Evesen and Berenbusch are also the villages of his baptismal sponsors.

We know the closest Lutheran Church closest to Evesen and Berenbusch was Petzen
(See: link:https://www.meyersgaz.org/place/10466052[Meyer's Gazetteer]). Furthermore the bottom of the
xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[] mentions the family's parish and its acting pastor:

[quote]
____ 
Petzen d. 23sten September 1850
Pede vacanti
der vikarirende Prediger

Fürstentum Schaumburg Lippe.

pass:[L. Schwerdtmann von Bergkirchen.]
____

This translates as:

[quote]
____
Petzen, the 23rd of September 1850  
During a pastoral vacancy  
the acting preacher  

Principality of Schaumburg-Lippe  

pass:[L. Schwerdtmann von Bergkirchen.]
(signatory)
____

The acting pastor for Petzen was L. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen, and Petzen was the parish where the Krückeberg
were members, and where their and their ancestor's original birth, baptism, confirmation, marriage and death and
burial records are found.

As of November 30, 2025, we only have online access to the Petzen church registers prior to 1827, but those after
1827 are only is only available at the Lower Saxony State Archive in Bückeburg. So we must rely on the
xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[] because it states it is derived from the original Petzen church records.
The xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[] begins "Laut Angabe des hiesigen Kirchenbuchs..." or "According to
the local church register...". 

While we do have the two Krückeberg biographies who were founders of Immanuel Lutheran,
Decatur, Indiana

. xref:immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[]
. xref:immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-02.adoc[]

neither mentions the source of their information. On the other hand, the xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[]
does. It begins: Laut Angabe des hiesigen Kirchenbuchs ("according to the local church records").

Furthermore the biography of xref:immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-02.adoc[], the first child of Carl Friedrich, gives
the father's name of *"Carl Gottlieb"* and not *"Carl Friedrich"*.
