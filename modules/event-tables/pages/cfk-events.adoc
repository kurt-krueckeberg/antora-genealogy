= Carl Friedrich Krückeberg (b. 1807) Event Tables
:page-role: very-wide

== Introduction

The xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[] tells us that his parents lived in
"Beerenbusch" (more typically spelled *Berenbsuch*), where they were tenants. His mother was from
Evensen [sic] (Evesen), which is just south of Berenbusch. Evesen and Beerenbusch are all the
residences of his baptismal sponsors.

We know the closest Lutheran Church closest to Evesen and Berenbusch was Petzen (See: footnote myersgaz.com).

Furthermore *Petzen* is mentioned at the bottom of the  xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[]:

[quote]
____ 
Petzen d. 23sten September 1850
Pede vacanti
der vikarirende Prediger

Fürstentum Schaumburg Lippe.

L. Schwerdtmann von Bergkirchen.
____

which means:

[quote]
____
Petzen, the 23rd of September 1850  
During a pastoral vacancy  
the acting preacher  

Principality of Schaumburg-Lippe  

L. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen
(signatory)
____

The acting pastor for Petzen was L. Schwerdtmann of Bergkirchen. Petzen was the parish
were the Krückeberg were members, where their original birth, baptism, confirmation, marriage
and death and burial records are found.

But we don't as of November 30, 2025, have acces online to Petzen church records after 1827.
So we turn to the records derived from the original records, which are:

. xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[]
. xref:immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[]
. xref:immanuel:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-02.adoc[]
