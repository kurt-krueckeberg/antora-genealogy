Those Krueckebergs who know they originate from northeast Adams County,
Indiana, might not know that Krueckeberg is simply an equivalent spelling of
Krückeberg. The German vowel "ü" can be also written "ue"; the vowel
"ä" can be written "ae", and the vowel "ö" as "oe". So Müller can be
spelled Mueller; Schäfer can be spelled Schaefer, and Söst can be spelled
Soest.

But since the vowels sounds of "ü", "ö" and "ä" don't exist in English, the
pronounciations of surnames like Krueckeberg, Oestermeyer, Soest, etc. have been
anglicized.
