= Research Request: Non-Church Evidence for Krückeberg of No. 6 Berenbusch (1803)
:page-role: wide

== Summary of Known Facts

* Individual of interest: **Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg**, recorded as **Hausherr**.
* Wife: **Christine Philippine Techtmeÿer**.
* Child: **Sophie Wilhelmine Krückeberg**, baptized **24 January 1803** at **No. 6 Berenbusch**.
* Baptism record reference: xref:petzen:PET-B-1803a.adoc[].
* Research goal: To discover what **non-Kirchenbuch records** can reveal about the **Krückeberg family**, especially details that church books do not record (tenure, landholding, economic status, succession, and household continuity).

== Key Clarification

The aim is **not** to reconstruct the history of Haus Nr. 6 itself.  
The purpose is to uncover **additional information about the Krückebergs** that can only be found in secular, house-organized records. These sources may reveal:

* the family's **tenure type** (Kolon, Kötter, Brinksitzer, etc.)  
* **land parcels**, rents, and obligations  
* **insurance valuations**  
* **tax status** and assessed wealth  
* **inheritance or succession** at No. 6  
* whether earlier or later occupants were also Krückebergs  

Because these records are arranged **by house number**, No. 6 Berenbusch is the necessary key to locating non-church information.

== Research Request (For On-Site Researcher)

Please locate and examine records for **No. 6 Berenbusch** (ca. 1780–1830) in the following administrative series.
*Note:* Precise archival signatures for these volumes are **not fully published** and must be identified on site
with archivist assistance.

1. **Brandversicherungen / Brandkassenkataster**  
   Insurance registers listing householders and buildings; may show the Krückeberg name, valuations, and year-to-year changes.

2. **Kontributions- und Schatzungsregister**  
   Taxpayer and valuation lists; helpful for identifying tenure, household wealth, and Krückeberg predecessors or successors.

3. **Lagerbücher / Meier- und Grundregister**  
   Registers of hereditary rights, obligations, land parcels, and changes of possession tied to No. 6.

4. **Kolonen- und Hausnummernverzeichnisse**  
   Long-running lists of householders by house number; may extend the Krückeberg line backward or forward.

Please extract all entries associated with **No. 6 Berenbusch**, with emphasis on identifying the **Krückebergs**,
their tenure, their economic standing, and any succession patterns.

== Research Plan

1. **Confirm Household Identity**  
   Match all entries for **No. 6 Berenbusch** with xref:petzen:PET-B-1803a.adoc[].

2. **Determine Tenure Category**  
   Identify whether Jobst Heinrich was Kolon, Kötter, Brinksitzer, or another type of hereditary tenant.

3. **Trace Predecessors and Successors**  
   Determine whether earlier or later holders of No. 6 were Krückebergs, related kin, or in-laws.

4. **Assess Economic Status**  
   Examine valuations, rents, and landholdings to understand the family’s social and economic position.

5. **Construct a Household Timeline**  
   Create a chronological sequence of occupancy at No. 6 that clarifies generational continuity or household transitions.

6. **Incorporate Findings**  
   Add all non-Kirchenbuch evidence to the Krückeberg family reconstruction with precise cross-references.

== Expected Outcome

A fuller picture of **Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg** and his household, including their **tenure, landholding, tax
status, and succession**, as revealed by secular administrative records not reflected in the Kirchenbücher.
