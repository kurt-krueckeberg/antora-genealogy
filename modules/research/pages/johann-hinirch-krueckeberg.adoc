. Jobst Krückeberg is the father of Sophie Maria born in 1741. Yet her birth record
reads: "Kruekeberg in Behrenbusch" suggesting there is only one.
. Several records don't give the father's or mother's given name. Examples:
..  "Krückeberg aus Berenbusch" suggest this might be the only Krückeberg family in Berenbusch.
.. The 1744 baptism of Johann Heinrich says: "Krückeberg ein Söhnlein...". It doesn't mention
the village. Most records on the page do mention the father's name. A few on this page and the 
prior--double check this--don't give the father's name, possibly suggesting there was only
one Krückeberg family.
