documented in the following files. We must keep in mind though, that
these files only record property owners, not tenants.

Berenbusch – the ‚Brinksitzer‘ properties belonged to the Prince and tax
has to be paid to him, as is noted on the right side of this page. Among
the property owners there is no Krückeberg, nor do the names of his
predecessors Kuhlmann and Eggerding (compare below, protocol) appear.

*NLA BU L 2 L Nr. 27/41* Protokolle über die Befragung der einzelnen
Hofbesitzer in +
Evesen, Berenbusch, Nordholz und Rusbend im Rahmen der +
Generallandesvisitation (Landesvermessung) 1743-1744

The householders are each asked 40 questions. The questions are
unfortunately not documented in this file, so we get to know only the
answers. Many of the questions were negated by recording either ‚nescit‘
(he does not know) or ‚cessat‘ (does not apply, falls away), but we do
not know what the topics were that this refers to. Also, some questions
are answered by ‚ut reliqui‘, which means he had the same conditions as
all the others.
