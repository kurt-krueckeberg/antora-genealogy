= Other High-value Documents for Genealogy Research of Petzen / Berenbusch

== 1. *Hofakten (farm files) — TOP PRIORITY*

* For *Stätte Nr. 10 Berenbusch*
* Held in the *Niedersächsisches Landesarchiv – Staatsarchiv Bückeburg*
* Track:
** ownership succession
** inheritances
** disputes

👉 This is the closest thing to a continuous record for your exact property

=== Where:

* *fonds K (Hofkammerarchiv)*
* Also sometimes in *fonds L (Amt / local administration)*

=== What to request/search:

* Keywords:
** _Hofsachen_
** _Kolonate_
** _Stätte Nr. 10 Berenbusch_
** _Erbzins / Meierbrief_

👉 These files track:

* ownership succession
* inheritance divisions
* obligations tied to the farm


== 2. *Brandkassenregister (fire insurance)*

* Property-based listings by house/farm
* Often survive for Schaumburg-Lippe

👉 Good for confirming who held the farm at specific dates

=== Where:

* Usually in *fonds L (financial / police / administrative)*

=== Search terms:

* _Brandkasse_
* _Brandversicherungsregister_
* _Gebäudeverzeichnis_

👉 Expect:

* lists by house/farm
* building descriptions
* owner names


== 3. *Kataster / Flurbücher (cadastral records)*

* Early 19th c. land surveys
* Parcel-level ownership in Berenbusch

👉 Useful for mapping and verifying land tied to your ancestor

=== Where:

* *fonds L (19th-century administration)*

=== What to look for:

* Early 1800s surveys (Napoleonic/post-Napoleonic period)
* Parcel registers for:
** Berenbusch
** Petzen parish

👉 These are often paired with:

* maps (separate map collections)


== 4. *Kontributionsregister (tax lists)*

* Periodic lists of residents by holding

👉 Can confirm:

* continuity of *Nr. 10*
* economic status

=== Where:

* *fonds L (Amt Bückeburg or regional tax offices)*

=== Search terms:

* _Kontribution_
* _Schatzung_
* _Steuerregister_

👉 Typically organized:

* by village → by holding


== 5. *Militär-Stammrollen*

* Only for applicable males
* Sometimes includes Petzen residents

=== Where:

* *fonds L (Militärwesen)*

=== Terms:

* _Stammrollen_
* _Aushebung_


== 6. *Guardianship / poor relief files*

* Only if triggered by:
** death
** debt
** minors

=== Where:

* *fonds L (Justiz / Vormundschaft)*


== Search Strategy (Arcinsys)

* Use *Arcinsys*
* It is *finding-aid level*, not full-text
* Search by:
** *German subject terms*
** *administrative unit (Amt Bückeburg / Petzen)*

=== Example search terms:

* `Berenbusch Kataster`
* `Berenbusch Brandkasse`
* `Petzen Kontribution`
* `Kolon Berenbusch Nr 10`
* `Hof Berenbusch`

👉 Then follow:

* *fonds → series → file*

* General "searchable" databases → no meaningful coverage
