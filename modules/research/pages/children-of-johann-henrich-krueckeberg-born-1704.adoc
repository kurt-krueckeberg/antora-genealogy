= Who are the parents the Krückeberg Children Born Between 1728 to 1750

There were seven Krückeberg children born between 1728 and 1750, but we have
only one recorded Krückeberg marriage that we know of prior to 1728, that of
xref:petzen:petzen-band1b-image36.adoc[Johann Henrich Krückeberg to Anna Marie
Vahlsing on September 28, 1727].

== Jobst Krückeberg

A Jobst Krückeberg was the father of Sophia Maria Kruekeberg, who was baptiyed in 1741; we know this from
her xref:petzen:petzen-band2-image239.adoc[death record] where her father is listed.
