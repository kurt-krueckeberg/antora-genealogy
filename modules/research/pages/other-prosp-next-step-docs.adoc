== 1. The *single best "`needle-finder`" source*

In those documents in the table above, the best prospective one to
investigate is *NLA BU, L 2, L Nr. 27/54* _Spezialvermessung der Dörfer
Evesen, Berenbusch und Nordholz (1734–1742)_

This is the *only item in your list* that:

* Covers *Berenbusch specifically*
* Falls *before* the 1743–44 interrogations
* Is a *village-level, nominative survey*
* Likely lists *Stätten numbers + holders*

If Johann Heinrich Krückeberg appears here for *No. 10*, this pushes his
tenure *back before 1743*. If someone else appears, you have a *named
predecessor*.

So in terms of _direct, decisive evidence_ for *who held No. 10 and
when*:

➡️ *Yes — this is the one with the highest payoff.*

These won’t usually give you the _first_ holder, but they help define
the *transition window*:

=== a)

*NLA BU, L 2, L Nr. 27/28 (1737)* _Erfassung der Haus- und Hofbesitzer
im Amt Bückeburg_

This is useful because:

* It gives a *snapshot just before 1743*
* If No. 10 is listed under a different name, you have a *terminus post
quem*

But it’s broader and less detailed than the Spezialvermessung.

=== b)

*NLA BU, L 2, L Nr. 27/41 (1743–44)* The interrogations you already
know.

This anchors the *latest possible date* for Krückeberg’s tenure.


== 3. Why most of the others won’t help

They do *not* usually list:

* Individual Berenbusch Stätten

* Or transitions of possession

So for your specific question (first tenure of No. 10):

➡️ They are not worth the effort.
