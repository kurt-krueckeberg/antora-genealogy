== What was the Family of Origin of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg (1807-1879)?

The Immanuel Lutheran biography of xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[Johann Heinrich Krückeberg] serves
as a cross reference to his origianl Petzen xref:petzen:PET-B-1806a.adoc[] record, which confirms his 12 August 1806
birthdate and further clarifies the complete names of his parents and baptism sponsors, supplies the family's residence
number of #10 in Berenbusch, as well as his father's occupation.

== Constructing the Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg Family

Using these details as our guide, we look for all records of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg's family of origin.

We find that his father, Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg (1806-1879), had four children. His xref:petzen:PET-D-1822b.adoc[death and
burial record] of Oct. 1822 states that his survivors were: *"a widow and four children, namely three daughters and
one son".* The birth record of his last child and first son, Johann Heinrich, states that he is the *"fourth child
and the first son"*. The four children documented children of Jobst Heinrich in the Petzen church records are:

. xref:petzen:PET-B-1792a.adoc[Philippine Carolina Christine, born 1 Feb. 1792]
. xref:petzen:PET-B-1798a.adoc[Anne Christina Eleonore, born 14 Feb. 1798]
. xref:petzen:PET-B-1803a.adoc[Sophie Wilhelmine, born 24 Jan 1803]
. xref:petzen:PET-B-1806a.adoc[Johann Heinrich, born 12 Aug 1806]

We know from the xref:nla:146:doc2.adoc#summary[second document] in the Rentkammer
(fiscal chamber) case file
link:https://www.arcinsys.niedersachsen.de/arcinsys/detailAction?detailid=v4979522[NLA
BU K 2 B Nr. 146] found in the Bückeburg regional archive that Jobst Heinrich's
father was an *Ackervogt*, a *local agricultural supervisory official* appointed by
the territorial administration or manor to *oversee the use and management of arable
land* within a village or district. He was the official who made sure that *fields
were used correctly, agricultural rules were followed, and land-related obligations
were met* by those who held or used farmland.

The reference to Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg’s father as an Ackervogt indicates that he
was a mature and experienced agricultural practitioner, entrusted with supervisory
responsibilities over farming activities. While this does not establish the scale of
his own holdings, it strongly suggests long-term practical experience in agriculture
and local land management.
