== Introduction

The Immanuel Lutheran biography of xref:jhk-1806-events.adoc#event-table[Johann Heinrich Krückeberg] serves
as a cross reference to the Petzen church records. It directs to the xref:petzen:PET-B-1806a.adoc[] record,
which confirms his birth date, his parents names (which are further clarified), his baptismal sponsors, and the
family's residence. The biography also guides us us to the xref:petzen:PET-C-1820a.adoc[] record. Then using
the details in these two records--the parent's names, their residence, the father's occupation, and any other
family attributes--as a guide, we look for other children born to this couple.

Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg (1806-1879) had four children. His xref:petzen:PET-D-1822b.adoc[death and
burial record] of Oct. 1822 states that his survivors were: *"a widow and four children, namely three daughters and
one son".* The birth record of his last child and first son, Johann Heinrich, states that he is the *"fourth child
and the first son"*. The four children documented children of Jobst Heinrich according to the Petzen church
records are:

. xref:petzen:PET-B-1792a.adoc[Philippine Carolina Christine, born 1 Feb. 1792]
. xref:petzen:PET-B-1798a.adoc[Anne Christina Eleonore, born 14 Feb. 1798]
. xref:petzen:PET-B-1803a.adoc[Sophie Wilhelmine, born 24 Jan 1803]
. xref:petzen:PET-B-1806a.adoc[Johann Heinrich, born 12 Aug 1806]
