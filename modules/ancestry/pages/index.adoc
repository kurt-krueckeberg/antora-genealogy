= Krückeberg Ancestry

There are three documents that tell us the place of origin of the Krückebergs who settled in Adams County,
Indiana, in 1849 and 1850: the xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[], the xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[]
and the xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[]. All three give Berenbusch (which they spell in various ways) in the principality of Schaumburg-Lippe as
that place. 

Johann Heinrich Krückeberg was the brother-in-law of another Immanuel Lutheran founder, Heinrich Wilhelm Weiland.
Both of their families traveled together to the U.S. aboard the same ship. As the
xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Weiland.adoc[] tells us, he was from Evesen, which was located just south of Berenbusch.

The closest Lutheran parish to Berenbusch and Evesen was Petzen, which is mentioned in all ??? four/three
documents.
