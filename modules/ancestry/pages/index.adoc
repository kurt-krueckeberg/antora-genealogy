= The Ancestry of the Krückebergs of Union Township, Adams County, Indiana

In 1849 the family of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg emigrated from the village of Berenbusch, in Schaumburg-Lippe,
and settled in Adams County, Indiana. In 1850, the family of Carl Friedrich Krückeberg also from
Berenbusch likewise emigrated and joined them in Adams County.

The trail of documentary evidence that reveals the place of origin of the two Krückeberg families who settled
in Adams County, Indiana, in 1849 and 1850, leads backs to two documents:

* the xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[family certificate of the immigrant Carl Friedrich
Krückeberg]footnote:[Family certificate of the emigrant Carl Friedrich Krückeberg (Schaumburg-Lippe), typewritten copy;
provenance unknown. Internal evidence shows Petzen parish certification by the acting pastor L. Schwerdtmann from Bergkirchen. My late father’s first cousin Melvin Krueckeberg supplied me a copy; he had received it from
his (and my late father’s) first cousin, Harry Frederick Krueckeberg, who believes his late father prepared the typescript (per email).], and
* the Immanuel Lutheran, Decatur Indiana, founder's xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[biography of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg]footnote:[Immanuel
Lutheran Church (Decatur, Indiana), church register, 1850–1905, biographical entry for Johann Heinrich
Krückeberg. The register was obtained on DVD directly from the church.]

The family of Carl Friedrich Krückeberg emigrated from the village of Berenbusch, Schaumburg-Lippe, in the fall of
1850.
