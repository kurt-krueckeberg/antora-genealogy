= Krückeberg Ancestry

There are three documents that tell us the place of origin of the Krückebergs who settled  in Adams County,
Indiana, in 1849 and 1850 originate: the xref:derived-church-records:FS-CFK.adoc[], xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[]
and xref:immanuel:ROOT:DEC-BIO-Krueckeberg-01.adoc[].

All three douments give Berenbusch (which they spell in various ways) in the principality of Schaumburg-Lippe as
the place of origin.
