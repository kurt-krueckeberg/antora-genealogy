== Transliteration and Translation


.Transliteration
[literal.bordered,subs="verbatim,quotes"]
....
25) 10t Octobr ist begraben Johann Heinrich Blecke
aus Evesen ein Ehemann. Sein Krankhiet was das bösartige Fieber. Sein Alter
34¼ Jahr.
....

.Translation
[literal.bordered,subs="verbatim,quotes"]
....
25) 10th October Johann Heinrich Blecke was buried a married man from
    Evesen was buried.
His illnes was the malignant fever. His age was 34¼ years.
....
