[IMPORTANT]
====
It is very possible that *Jobst* is incorrect, a scribal error, and her father was *Johann Heinrich*. 
No. 10 Berenbusch was the home of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg. The first mention of Johann Heinrich
living at no. 10 Berenbusch was on 27 August 1773 when a xref:petzen:PET-D-1773a.adoc[stillborn son
of Johann Heinrich Krückeberg "aus Berenbusch no. 10" was buried].
