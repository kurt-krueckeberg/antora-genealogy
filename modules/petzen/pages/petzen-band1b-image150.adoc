= 1734 Krückeberg Small Daughter Burial
:page-role: wide

image::petzen-band1b-img150.png[title="Krückeberg Töchterlein begraben (Click to Enlarge),link=self]

[NOTE]
====
In this burial entry, "ein Töchterlein" means a small/young daughter of H.
Krückeberg from Berenbusch. The pastor does not give her personal name.
In the Petzen church book, stillborn children are usually marked as "todtgeboren"
in the baptism/birth section; since that word is not used here, this Töchterlein
was born alive but died in early childhood. Whether she was baptized cannot be
told for certain from the burial entry alone. Therefore, the possible burial
record should be looked for.
====
