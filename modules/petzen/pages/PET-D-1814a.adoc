= 1814 Ilse Marie Werkmeister Death

Ilse Marie Werkmeister, nee Möller, was the mother-in-law of Carl Friedrich
Gottlieb Krückeberg, who married her daugher.

She died at age 73 as a Leibzüchterin at n. 11 Evesen. She was a widow.

[.narrow]
