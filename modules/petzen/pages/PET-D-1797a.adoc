= 1797 Anne Marie Philippine Wilharm Death
:page-role: wide

[.narrow]
== Citation

The death record of Anne Marie Philippine Wilharm is found on
<<PET-D-1797a, image 235>> of the link:https://archion.de[Archion] digital
images of the _Verzeichnis der Getauften, Konfirmierten, Getrauten und
Gestorbenen_.

[NOTE]
====
She died the day after giving xref:PET-B-1779a.adoc[birth to her first child, Hans Heinrich
Wilharm], who had the same name as his father.
====
