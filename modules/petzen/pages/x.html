1731 Heinrich Arend Vahlsing Death

Citation

The 3 Jan. 1730 burial record of Heinrich (Arends) Vahlsing is found on image 147 of the Petzen Kirchenbuch "Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784".

Image

Figure 1. Burial record for Heinrich Arends Vahlsing

Transliteration and Translation

Transliteration
Anno 1731

den 3t. Januarÿ ist Henrich Vahlsing gewesener
32 jähriger Schäfer auß dem Hochgr[äflichen]
Vorwerde Höckersau begraben seines alters 63 Jahr
Hochgräflichen Vorwerde Höckersau

In this 1731 Petzen death record, “Hochgräflichen Vorwerde Höckersau” refers to a manorial outlying farm (Vorwerk) belonging to the high comital (i.e., ruling count’s) estate, located at or called Höckersau*.

A precise breakdown:

  • Hochgräflich(en) Literally “high-comital.” This denotes ownership by the reigning Graf (Count) of Schaumburg-Lippe. In parish records, this adjective signals sovereign or comital domain property, not a private noble or peasant holding.

  • Vorwerde / Vorwerk An outlying estate or farm administratively attached to a main manor or domain (often supplying grain, livestock, or labor). Vorwerke were commonly staffed by shepherds, farmhands, and overseers rather than independent farmers.

  • Höckersau The specific place-name of that Vorwerk. In Schaumburg-Lippe usage, such names often designated a small settlement, estate complex, or cleared area associated with comital landholding.

Meaning in context

Henrich Vahlsing was a shepherd employed at the comital Vorwerk Höckersau*, i.e., a shepherd in service to the Count’s domain, not a village farmer or householder in Petzen itself.

Smooth English rendering

A historically faithful translation would be:

“from the High-Comital Vorwerk of Höckersau”

or slightly more explanatory:

“from the comital outlying estate (Vorwerk) at Höckersau”

This phrasing accurately conveys status (sovereign ownership), function (estate farm), and location (Höckersau) as understood in early-18th-century Schaumburg-Lippe records.

See [Höckersau-Sandfurt](https://www.meyersgaz.org/place/10808032)

Translation
In the year 1731,

On 3 January, Henrich Vahlsing, a shepherd, from the Count’s outlying
estate farm at Höckersau, aged 63 years, was buried

Translated into English, “high-comital Vorwerk of Höckersau” is therefore best understood as “the outlying estate of Höckersau belonging to the count,” with Vorwerk left untranslated to preserve its specific administrative meaning.
