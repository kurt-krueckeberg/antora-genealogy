== Citation

The birth of Caroline Sophie Weiland is entry 4 on <<PET-B-1808a, image 99,
pages 192-193>> of the Petzen Kirchenbuch volume "Verzeichnis der
Getauften, Konfirmierten, Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1785-1827."

[NOTE]
.Challenges in this entry
====
* Hasufrau definition
Thode's define `Hausfrau` as:
  "wife; (modern) housewife; landlady"

* The occupation of Friedrich Wilhelm Weiland is hard to discren. However,
  five other fathers in this image have the same occupation.  The most
legiblely written of these is entry six, which must be `Ackersmann`
(despite the 3^rd^ letter more closely resembling `l` or a `t`).
====
