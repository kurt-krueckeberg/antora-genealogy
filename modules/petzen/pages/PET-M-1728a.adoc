The October 21, 1728, marriage of Henrich Vahlsing to Anna Margaretha Bruns
is on <<PET-M-1728a, image 37>>, the right-hand page, of the Petzen 
"Verzeichnis der Getrauten und Gestorbenen 1641-1784".
