[IMPORTANT]
====
While the mother's name is not given on the baptism record, the male baptismal sponsor is *Rösener*. On 29 Sep. 1758, Johann
Heinrich Krückeberg from Berenbusch married Ilse Margarethe Rösener from Evesen as their
xref:petzen:PET-M-1758a.adoc[] record shows.
====

== Citation

The March 24, 1765 baptism of Jobst Heinrich Krückeberg is on <<PET-B-1765a, image 211>>, left page, fourth entry from the top, of
the "Verzeichnis der Getauften und Konfirmierten 1641-1784" (Register of Baptism and Confirmed of the Petzen Parish, 1641-1784).
