[IMPORTANT]
====
Not only was *Johann Tönnies Eggerding* a baptismal sponsor for Johann
Heinrich's son, Johann Friedrich, he was also the former owner of the
holding of no. 10 Berenbusch, which we know Johann Heinrich was living at
by 1747 and perhaps earlier.

The secular document *Namensverzeichnis sämtlicher Kolonen der Ämter
Bückeburg und Arensburg, mit Nummerierung der sämtlichen Häuser ("List of
names of all Kolons of the offices of Bückeburg and Arensburg, with
numbering of the all houses")* shows us that in 1747 Johann Heinrich
Krückeberg was the owner of the holding no. 10 Berenbusch and Johann
Tönnies Eggerding was the prio owner. 

This is mentioned xref:nla:140:page-17.adoc[page 17 of the volume].
====

.Explanations
****
[NOTE]
====
The father's name appears to come first with the surname ending in "s". In contemporary German, an "s"
after someone's name, usually the first name, signifies the genitive case (possessive case); for example,
`Paul's Car` would be `Pauls Auto`. Translation literally word-for-word without changing the grammer
yields:

7 July is Johann Heinrich Krückeberg's
little son Johann Friedrich baptized. Godfathers
have been Johann Tönnies Eggerding und H Herman Wiese
====

NOTE: Anno 1728 (Year 1728) appears at the top of on the prior page. Thus these baptism
records continue on with those in 1728, and the Krückeberg son is the 18^th^ baptism of
1728.

NOTE: Unlike the other baptism entries where the date appears first, the date of *7 Julÿ* is
written above the father's name, apparently the pastor forgot to start the entry with the date and had to insert it. 
****
