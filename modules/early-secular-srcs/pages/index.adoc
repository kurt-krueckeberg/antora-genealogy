= Early Secular Records

== Johann Heinrich Krückebereg's at no. 10 Berenbusch in 1747

A number of xref:nla:ROOT:index.adoc[secular records] in the Bückeburg regional department of the
Lower Saxony state archives mention the Krückeberg's of Berenbusch. 

They are mentioned in several Rentkammer case files, and in one early
document from 1747, the xref:nla:140:page-17.adoc[List of names of all Kolons of the offices of
Bückeburg and Arensburg, with numbering of the all houses], we learn that
Johann Heinrich Krückeberg is by 1747 a Brinksitzer of no. 10 Berenbusch.

Furthermore the prior owner of the holding, Johann Tönnies Eggerding, was
also a xref:petzen:PET-B-1728a.adoc[baptism sponsor of Johann Heinrich's
son, Johann Friedrich], who was born in 1728.
