= 1798 Christine Marie Schwier Baptism
:page-role: wide

== Citation

The baptism of Christine Marie Schwier occured on 13 May 1798. It is <<FRI-B-1798a, image 138, page 259>> of the
Frille "Verzeichnis der Getauften, Getrauten, Gestorbenen 1758-1804".

== Transliteration and Translation

[NOTE]
====
*"Eodem"* means *"on the same days"* as the entry above, whose date is *13 Mai*.

Thus, the baptism date is *13 May 1798*.
====
