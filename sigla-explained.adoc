= Sigla Explained

== Original Records

The general format for most of the sigla representing original records is LLL-E-YYYYx, for exammple:
*PET-B-1744a*.

The scheme for original records is:

* LLL--is a 3-letter location prefix shown in the table:
+
[cols="1,2,6"]
|===
|3-letter Prefix|Location|Meaning

|BUC
|Buchholz
|Parish in Buchholz, "Germany"

|CIN
|Cincinnati
|Deutsche Evangelisch-Lutherische und Reformierte Kirche von Cincinnati (German Evangelical Lutheran and Reformed Church of 
Cincinnati)

|FRI
|Frille
|Parish in Frille, "Germany"

|PET
|Petzen
|Parish in Petzen, Schaumburg-Lippe

|DEC
|Decatur
|Immanuel Lutheran Church, Decatur, Indiana

|NDC
|Cincinnati
|Norddeutsche Evangelisch-Lutherische Kirche, Cincinnati (North German Lutheran Church of Cincinnati)

|WAL
|Wallensen
|Wallsensen Parish, "Germany"

|WIN
|Windheim
|Windheim Parish, "Germany"
|===
* E--is a one-letter church ceremony code shown in the table below:
+
|===
|Code|Ceremony

|B
|Birth/Baptism

|C
|Confirmation

|M
|Marriage

|D
|Burial/Death
|===
* YYYY--is the year of the ceremony
* x--is a,b,c, d, etc, representing separate indvidual ceremonies from one another.
